Introducing Q8botOne

The next generation of Q8bot, built for hobbyists, educators, and researchers.

No wires or cables during assembly—everything is directly integrated onto the central PCB.
This improves electrical reliability, reduces size and weight, and simplifies servicing.

Q8botOne uses ROBOTIS DYNAMIXEL smart actuators—a step up from hobby servos, with fast, precise motion and sensor feedback.
